## Material routes

**314 COMP: Arbitrum to Ethereum to exchange-labelled addresses.** Six vault payments of 10, 40, 70, 50, 60 and 84 COMP reached the same recipient during March–May 2024. Each was withdrawn through the Arbitrum gateway. Child withdrawal position, caller, destination and complete message bytes match the successful Ethereum execution and exact COMP payment. The recipient then transferred each amount to an Etherscan-labelled Binance deposit address, which swept the amounts to another Binance-labelled address. This establishes the public route, not exchange account ownership, credited balances, subsequent trades or reimbursement. ## Announcements and reopening

No community identities or private messages were used in this package.

The [workflow register](comp-workflow.json) distinguishes checked work from evidence boundaries. Reopen on fuller BSC/Base history, another vault, public pinned Fusion balances/restrictions, authenticated claim-conversion or settlement records, a bridge/peg backing discrepancy, or an independently verified exchange-account attribution. Custody and beneficial ownership cannot be concluded from these public routes alone.
